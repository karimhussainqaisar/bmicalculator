About BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. It is used to categorize individuals into weight status groups: Underweight (<18.5), Normal weight (18.5–24.9), Overweight (25–29.9), and Obese (≥30). The Ponderal Index (PI) is a similar measure but uses height cubed, providing an alternative perspective on body composition.

Health Risks: Being overweight increases risks for diabetes, heart disease, and certain cancers. Being underweight may indicate malnutrition, osteoporosis, or underlying conditions like anorexia nervosa. Consult a healthcare provider for personalized advice.

Limitations: BMI does not distinguish between muscle and fat, nor does it account for age, sex, ethnicity, or body fat distribution. It is a screening tool, not a diagnostic measure.
